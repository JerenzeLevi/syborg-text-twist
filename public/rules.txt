SYBORG TEXT TWIST — RULES
=========================

GOAL
Each round scrambles a hidden base word. Tap letter tiles to spell any real
word of 3+ letters hidden inside it, then hit Enter. Find as many sub-words
as you can before time runs out — finding a word the same length as the full
base word is the biggest prize of the round.

CONTROLS
- Click tiles (or type letters on your keyboard) to build a guess
- Enter   — submit the current guess
- Back    — remove the last letter
- Clear   — wipe the current guess
- Twist   — reshuffle the tiles
- Hint    — reveals the first letter of the longest unsolved word (costs 25 points)

SCORING
- 3-letter word: 10 pts
- 4-letter word: 20 pts
- 5-letter word: 40 pts
- 6-letter word: 70 pts
- 7+ letter word: 15 pts per letter
- Every correct word also adds a few seconds to the clock (longer words add more)
- Back-to-back correct words build a streak multiplier (up to 2.5x) — a wrong
  guess resets it
- Solving the full base word banks a one-time "Second Wind": the clock
  auto-refills to 120s the instant it would otherwise hit zero
- Hints cost 25 points each and only work if you can afford one

TIMING & PENALTIES
- Every round starts with 120 seconds on the clock
- Guessing a real dictionary word that isn't in this round's letter set: -3 seconds
- Guessing something that isn't a real word at all: -10 seconds
- Re-submitting a word you already found does nothing (no penalty, no points)
- Clearing every word in the round advances you straight to a fresh puzzle,
  carrying your score, time, and streak forward — Daily Challenge stays on
  its one shared puzzle instead

MODES (combinable via toggles on Home)
- Daily Challenge — everyone gets the same puzzle for the day (Manila time),
  for a fair leaderboard comparison
- Technical       — the base word is always IT/CS/BLIS/BSIS vocabulary
- Abyssal         — you get 3 lives; a wrong guess costs a life and 0 lives
  ends the round immediately
- Glitch          — the tile row jolts and the word-length bubbles scramble
  into fake numbers on a timer
- Blind           — tiles blur from memory after a few seconds, with periodic
  full blackouts

BADGES
- Master of Wind        — bank Second Wind (find the full base word) in 20 rounds in a row
- Wind Immunity         — finish 10 rounds in a row without ever needing Second Wind
- Hintless              — finish 5 rounds in a row without using a hint
- Bro thinks s/he's speed — clear every word in a round in under 5 seconds
- Speedrun!             — clear 20 rounds in a row within 2 minutes total
- Call ambulance but not for me — clear a round with under 3 seconds left
- King below The Apex; Levi / W-w-what a hacker! — land at #2 or #1 on the
  leaderboard, any mode
- A few other badges are secrets — you'll know when you've found one

LEADERBOARD
After a round ends, save your score under a name. Scores are tracked
separately per mode combination, and using the same name in the same mode
again offers to replace your lower score. A permanent top score belonging to
"Jerenze Levi Omandam" sits on the board for everyone to chase.
